## Pro Tips
<a id="pro-tips"></a>

- **Use a Strong Master Password** — A robust master password is essential for protecting your encrypted database. Consider using a mix of upper and lower case letters, numbers, and symbols.
- **Regularly Update Your Passwords** — Make it a habit to change your passwords periodically for enhanced security.
- **Organize by Categories** — Group your passwords by category (e.g., banking, social media) to make retrieval quicker and easier.
- **Backup Your Database** — Regularly back up your password database to avoid losing access in case of software issues.
- **Utilize Clipboard Feature Wisely** — When using the clipboard feature, remember to clear it after pasting to prevent unauthorized access to your passwords.
